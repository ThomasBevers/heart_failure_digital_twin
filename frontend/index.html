MODEL TRANSPARENCY

How this research twin works

A visual workspace for a patient-specific, closed-loop cardiovascular model.

01

Source measurements

Heart rate, blood pressure and LVEF establish the baseline. EDV and ESV are used when both are supplied; otherwise the backend derives reference volumes.

02

Calibration

The simulator calibrates contractility so its baseline reproduces the supplied LVEF, then solves a closed-loop circulation over repeated cardiac cycles.

03

Scenario

Controls perturb contractility, preload, afterload and heart rate on the fitted baseline. They do not alter the entered or extracted source values.

⚑

Interpretation. This interface surfaces research outputs only. It does not validate clinical suitability, automate diagnosis, or make treatment recommendations.

VOLUME RESOLUTION

How are ventricular volumes used?

If both EDV and ESV are entered or returned by the configured EchoNet volume estimator, the twin uses those reported volumes. If neither is available, the backend derives a reference ventricular volume from the other patient measurements. The API rejects a lone EDV or ESV to prevent ambiguous input.
